# Original Coucou artwork

EasyIsland is based on [Coucou](https://github.com/Louis-CFM/coucou) by Louis
Raillé. The [MIT License](LICENSE) covers the source code of both.

EasyIsland's own name, characters (Slime, Goccia and EasyTech), icons and
sounds are new and drawn or synthesised in code. None of Coucou's brand assets
ship with EasyIsland, and since 4 October 2026 none are left in this
repository's files.

The original Coucou artwork remains © 2026 Louis Raillé, all rights reserved,
and is **not** covered by the MIT License:

- the names **"Coucou"** and **"Mochi"** and the Mochi character;
- older assets that appear in this repository's git history (the Coucou icon,
  the Mochi sounds, the images in `docs/media/`, the prototype, animations and
  captures that were in `design/`).

Do not reuse them in a published app without the author's written permission.
