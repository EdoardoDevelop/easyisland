Getting started

The island

How it opens, what it holds and how to use it with the mouse and the keyboard.

#The character and its states

At rest the character sits in the corner you picked (top centre by default). It changes colour with the most urgent state: blue while an agent works, amber for a pending permission, red for an error, green when something finished well. When a tab is open it wears that integration's colour.

What you doWhat happens
Hover over the characterIt grows, hops, looks at you and waves
Click, or stay over it for a momentThe island opens, aligned to that side
Drag the characterIt moves where you drop it, even to another screen; the position is saved in the profile
Drag the bottom corner of the open islandWidth and height change up to the screen edges. Double-click: default sizes
Drag the open island by its top barIt stays there while open, then goes back to the character
Click the character several timesIt gets annoyed; three in a row and it gets dizzy
Leave the pointer on it for two secondsHearts
Drag a file onto the islandIt becomes a box, swallows it and asks what to do
Drag the character onto a folder in File Explorer or a desktop iconThe path lands in the chat field
Esc or ✕Closes the island at once
📌Keep open: the island won't close by itself until you remove it
— and ▢With Like an app on (Settings → Island): minimize and full screen. The island always has its icon on the taskbar (a click opens it) and, open, it shows in Alt+Tab
Stay openWith Stay open on (Settings → Island), when you open it yourself it does not close by itself, as with 📌
A window opens over the island (a picker, a program launched from the island…)The island goes behind it, without closing. It comes forward again when you click it, when a permission or a call arrives, or when it tucks away

#The menu on the left

In the open island, under the character:

  • Overview: every integration and agent as tiles, each with its state. A click opens the tab; drag to reorder.
  • Agents: coding agent sessions and tasks (see Coding agents and Tasks), with how many are working.
  • Chat: the chat with the AI engine.
  • Actions: your quick actions and suggestions for the app in use.
  • Files and screen: file drop, Tray, working folder, Circle and ask, Record (see Files and screen).
  • Your integrations, each with its logo or an emoji and a dot when there's news; custom integrations too.

At the top the character tells you what's going on in a speech bubble. Right-clicking inside the island opens a menu with the tab's and EasyIsland's actions.

#The field at the top

One field searches integrations, sessions, ⚡ actions and installed programs (Store apps too). Arrows to choose, Enter to open, Ctrl+Enter to ask the chat what you typed, "Search with Google" at the bottom, Esc to clear. It can be turned off in Settings → Look.

#Quick replies, with the island closed

With Quick replies on, next to the closed character you get:

  • who is working and what it's doing;
  • Deny / Allow on simple permissions (questions, plans and risky commands always open the full card);
  • Answer / Decline on 3CX calls and, during the call, the duration, Mute and Hang up;
  • during a Teams meeting Microphone, Video, Leave;
  • while recording, the red dot, the time and Stop.

#The bar of the app in use

Next to the closed character a bar can show actions for the app you're using (like a Touch Bar): your quick actions for that program and Keys actions that press shortcuts for you. Set it up in Settings → Quick actions.

#Cards that come by themselves

The island opens on its own when needed, following the profile's notification rules:

  • agents' permissions and questions (they stay until you answer; "Later" or Esc fold them away);
  • session finished with the last message and Continue;
  • incoming calls, starting meetings, alerts from integrations and widgets;
  • suggestions at the right moment: download finished, error copied, dev server started, copied password to clear from the clipboard (see Files and screen);
  • recaps: morning, end of day and the agents' weekly recap;
  • available updates.

#From the keyboard

KeysWhat it does
Ctrl+SpaceOpens the island (Overview, actions or chat)
Ctrl+Alt+KChat with the copied text or image already attached
Ctrl+Alt+HClipboard history
Ctrl+Alt+Shift+SCircle and ask
Ctrl+Alt+Shift+PGoes to the pending request: N deny, Y allow, S always, 1–9 pick
Ctrl+Alt+Shift+TBrings the session's app to the front
Win+Alt+AMenu of actions on the selected text, in any app
← → in the islandPrevious / next tab
Ctrl+N / Ctrl+PNew chat / keep open

Every shortcut can be changed in Settings → Quick actions. With the mouse the island doesn't take the keyboard, so it never steals it from the app you're using.

#The notification area icon

Open, Settings…, Profile ▸, In front of a client, Weekly recap, Pause (stops checks, automations and alerts) and Quit.
