Make it yours

Position and look

Where the character lives, how it shows itself, styles, characters, themes, sounds and language.

Everything is in Settings → Look. Sections with the purple label are saved in the active profile.

#Style

  • Windows 11 (default): island and Settings in grey like system apps, Windows' accent colour, line icons.
  • Classic: the original dark look with coloured icons.

#Position

  • Corner: top or bottom, left, centre or right. The island grows from there and its content stays aligned to that side.
  • Dragging the character: drop it anywhere; the side the island opens from is chosen automatically. Near an edge or the centre it snaps. Picking a corner again brings it back to the edge.
  • Screen: the main one, the one under the cursor or the one you dragged it to (if unplugged, it goes back to the main one).
  • The island opens: where the character is, or always at the top centre, the centre of the screen or the bottom centre.
  • Over the taskbar: the character may also sit over the taskbar.
  • Snap to edges: near an edge the background sticks to it with square corners.

#Sizes

Width and Minimum height of the open island, up to the screen edges (also by dragging its bottom corner). Defaults go back to 640 px.

#Behaviour

#Characters

Nine characters drawn in code, all with the same expressions:

  • Goccia (default): a glossy light-blue water spirit.
  • Slime: green jelly that wobbles.
  • EasyTech: a cube in the logo's colours, eyes on one side.
  • Geco: a gecko's head with amber eyes.
  • Fiamma: a layered little flame that sways.
  • Magma, Pietra, Roccia: pebbles with big eyes (lava, sprout, moss).
  • Foglia: a glossy leaf with veins.

The character reacts: it greets you at startup, celebrates when an agent finishes, worries about an error, looks at you when you hover. With the weather on it can have the sky behind it.

#Theme

The character's colour, the island's colour and opacity, background when closed (off: only the character stays).

#Sounds

All sounds are generated in code, no audio files: three separate volumes for alerts, interface and the character's emotions. Mute them in one go with the "sounds on / off" shortcut.

#Language

Settings → General → Language: like Windows, Italian or English. It applies after a restart (offered with Restart now); the chat also answers in that language, unless you write to it in another one.
