# 3CX
> section: Connecting services
> summary: The 3CX phone system in the island: phone on the PC, calls, contacts, team, chat and voicemail.

![3CX in the island](../media/threecx.png)

## Connecting
**Settings → Integrations → 3CX** (a **3CX V20** system). Two ways:
- **Extension and password** (recommended): EasyIsland signs in like the 3CX app, with the system's address (e.g. `https://company.my3cx.com:5001`), your extension or email and the web client password. No extra licence. It's how the 3CX web client works, not a documented interface: an update of the phone system might require an EasyIsland update. Two-step verification is not supported yet.
- **API client**: in the Admin Console, **Integrations → API → Add**, tick *3CX Call Control API Access* (and *Configuration API Access* for the contacts) and add your extension among the monitored ones. Requires an **8SC or higher** licence. Presence, history, team and chat aren't available this way.

## The tab, like a phone
On the left display, keypad, green key and device; on the right ongoing, recent and missed calls.
- **Calling**: type a number or search a name, company or colleague in the **contacts** and click the number; <kbd>Enter</kbd> calls the first result.
- **Device**: **From …** chooses where the call starts (3CX app for Windows, desk phone, smartphone, **This PC**) or *automatic*.
- **Presence** (Available, Away, Do not disturb…) from the menu next to "3CX".
- **Missed** and **Recent** calls.

## "This PC": EasyIsland is the phone
Choosing **This PC** as device, EasyIsland registers as the **WebRTC phone of your extension**:
- call and answer from the PC, with microphone and headset;
- **Mute**, keypad tones, **transfer** and **divert**;
- **ringtone** synthesised or one of your MP3s, on the audio device you choose.

## Incoming calls
The island opens on the 3CX tab with a sound and stays open while it rings, with **Answer** and **Decline**. With Quick replies on and the island closed, Answer and Decline appear **next to the character**, and during the call the duration with **Mute** and **Hang up**. With the tab's bell off only the dot stays. With "in front of a client" the caller's name doesn't show.

## Team, chat, voicemail, queues
With "Extension and password":
- **Team** in real time: colleagues' presence and **answer for them**;
- **Chat** with colleagues and groups;
- **Voicemail** with transcription;
- call **queues**.

## With automations
An incoming call can start an [automation](16-automations.md) (`{chiamante}`, `{numero}`), for example opening the client's record.

EasyIsland stays connected only with the integration on and not paused, and reconnects by itself if the connection drops. Numbers and names stay in memory.
