# Notion
> section: Connecting services
> summary: Notion tasks, notes and pages in the island, an editable mini Notion and the chat reading and writing your databases.

## Connecting
1. On notion.so → **Settings → Connections → Develop your own integrations** create an **internal integration** allowed to read, update and insert content.
2. Copy the token into **Settings → Integrations → Notion**.
3. In Notion, on the pages and databases you want to use, **Connections → add** your integration.

Settings suggest suitable databases by themselves: for **tasks** those with a date and a "done", for **notes** those named like notes, inbox, ideas or journal. Choosing the tasks database creates the Today, Week and All views.

## Tasks
The card shows tasks not done, overdue and today's first. The circle marks them done.
- **Views** (up to 6): a period (all, overdue and today, until Sunday, until month end, the next N days), the wanted values for selects, statuses and relations, sorting by due date or priority. Switch with one click.
- **+ Task**: title and due date (Today, Tomorrow, Monday or none), with the values of the view in use.
- **Postpone to tomorrow**, on hover.
- **Alerts**: a few minutes before a task with a time and for **new comments** on pinned pages and today's tasks. A click opens the Notion app.
- **Morning recap**: today's tasks in the morning card.

## Notes and pages
- **+ Note**: on a page it adds a paragraph with date and time; in a database a row.
- From selected text in any app (<kbd>Win</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>A</kbd>): **Note in Notion** and **Task in Notion**.
- **Pages** pinned as tiles.
- **Search** in the card and in the island's top field; **Ask the chat** attaches title and text.

## Mini Notion in the island
A tile, a search result or "all" tasks open **inside the island**:
- **pages** are drawn like in Notion (headings, lists, checkboxes, toggles, callouts, quotes, code, tables, columns, sub-pages) and **edited in place**: bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, code; <kbd>Enter</kbd> creates the block below, `/` and **+** pick the kind;
- **databases** have **Table**, **Board** (drag cards between columns), **List** and **Gallery**; statuses, selects, dates, checkboxes and texts can be changed;
- **Delete** wants a second click on "Sure?" and sends to **Notion's trash**, where it can be recovered;
- ↗ opens in the Notion app.

## Notion in the chat
Every engine with tools works on your Notion through EasyIsland's connection:
- a **map** of the databases (properties, options, relations) encrypted on the PC, refreshed every 6 hours or with **Sync**, so the chat doesn't waste time figuring out the structure;
- **search, read, query** databases (with totals already computed, also split by category);
- **add and change rows**, with values checked against the properties (never new options from a typo), with Allow / Deny;
- **delete a row** to the trash, only after your yes in the chat.

Examples: "how much did I spend on fuel in October?", "add a 2-hour intervention for Rossi today", "which tasks are due this week?".
